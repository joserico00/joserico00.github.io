The project

A notebook that turns luciferase reporter-assay spreadsheets into tidy data, per-condition statistics against a baseline, and publication-ready plots.

Implementation and context

Wet-lab spreadsheets arrive in wide plate layouts with two header rows. The notebook loads them with a reusable plate reader, reshapes to tidy form, computes summaries and baseline comparisons, and draws bar-with-points plots for the overview and each dose. Real lab data stays out of git; a synthetic example workbook demonstrates the flow.
