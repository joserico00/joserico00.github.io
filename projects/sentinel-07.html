The project

A first-person penetration-test write-up: Nmap enumeration from Kali Linux, public web disclosures turned into an SSH foothold, a SUID privilege escalation to root, and a pivot into a Docker-hosted MariaDB case database, with 30 flags recorded.

Implementation and context

The write-up explains the reasoning behind each step of the attack chain and ends with a catalog of all 30 flags that separates flags recovered by reading a file from exploits that were independently demonstrated. It was written after the CAHSI/GMiS 2026 Cyber Hackathon, a jeopardy-style capture-the-flag competition where my team placed 2nd in the Advanced Team Category.
