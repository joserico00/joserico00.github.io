The project

Energy analysis of TOP500 systems and fusion workloads, ending in a job scheduler that packs research projects under a 4.8 MW power cap.

Implementation and context

The bootcamp supplied the lesson text, starter code, and synthetic datasets. My work includes executed analyses, energy-per-job and science-per-kWh calculations, and a greedy scheduler that packs fusion jobs under 4.8 MW: 40% of the facility’s 12 MW budget. This complements my role mentoring students on the broader energy-constrained HPC project.

4.8 MW of a 12 MW facility budget

The scheduler’s constraint is the fusion-workload allocation, while the mentoring project considered the broader facility budget.
