The project

County-level analysis of the June 2016 Southwest heatwave, joining outage records, temperatures and Medicare data on electricity-dependent medical equipment.

Implementation and context

As a 2023 Intro to HPC Bootcamp participant, I worked with a team to analyze temperature and power outages during the June 2016 Southwest heatwave. We used Python, pandas, and Matplotlib on Perlmutter and considered the impact on people who rely on electricity-dependent medical equipment. The project combined time-series analysis and county-level mapping, and we presented our findings in a lightning talk. The notebooks build their maps when you run them rather than shipping saved copies, which keeps the repository small enough to browse on GitHub.
