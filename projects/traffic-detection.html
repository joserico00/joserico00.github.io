The project

A capture-aware IoT-23 pipeline that prevents preprocessing leakage and evaluates a neural network against a logistic-regression baseline.

Implementation and context

The current notebook holds entire captures apart for training, validation, and testing; fits preprocessing on the training set only; and trains class-weighted models. It reports precision, recall, F1, balanced accuracy, ROC AUC, and precision-recall AUC. Earlier ANN, CNN, LSTM, and Transformer experiments are preserved as historical coursework, with their limitations documented separately. Their saved scores are not presented as validated performance of the current pipeline.

Evaluation without capture overlap

The current pipeline keeps capture IDs disjoint and fits preprocessing only on training data. The repository documents historical results and their limitations; it does not claim a new benchmark score for this revision.
