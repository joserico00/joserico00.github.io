DEVOPS + CYBER-RESEARCH SOFTWARE

Infrastructure for cyber researchers

Containerized services for binary-feature search and language-model-assisted research-paper reading at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.

MY ROLE

DevOps Intern

ORGANIZATION

Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

WHEN

June – August 2024

The research setting

Cyber researchers need software infrastructure that makes specialized tools usable in day-to-day work. During my internship, I worked on application containerization and GitLab-based continuous integration and deployment in an Agile environment.

Binary-feature search

I built an Elasticsearch container to store binary features extracted by Ghidra BSim. The service supported security researchers querying malware-like samples at scale.

Ghidra BSim features→Elasticsearch container→Research queries

A language-model service for research reading

I also deployed a Docker-hosted Ollama service running Llama 3 to support the PaperQA PDF-reading application over LLNL research papers.

PaperQA↔Docker-hosted Ollama→Llama 3

Deployment and collaboration

I containerized cyber-research applications and integrated GitLab CI/CD pipelines into the team’s workflows. My contribution focused on supporting the applications and services that researchers used for binary analysis and document-based research.
