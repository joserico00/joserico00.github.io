The problem

The cooperative’s energy data is spread across Sol-Ark, FranklinWH, and sonnen portals. Monthly billing needs a consistent way to collect that data and apply the cooperative’s net-metering rules and per-site rates.

What I built

I built Solar Fleet Reporter, a local Python/Streamlit application that retrieves monthly energy data through APIs and Playwright-driven portal logins. The application combines collection, billing calculations, and report generation into one workflow.

Energy portals→Monthly site data→Billing rules→Member statements

From energy data to statements

  • Collect monthly data from three solar-platform providers.
  • Apply three net-metering billing cases and per-site rates.
  • Export CSV and Excel reports.
  • Generate printable homeowner statements.
  • Flag offline sites for follow-up.
Billing logic tested offline

The application separates billing calculations from portal connectors, with tests using synthetic customer data and representative payload shapes.

Why this work matters to me

This is volunteer software development for my community. The application gives the cooperative a repeatable process for preparing monthly billing and member statements across several energy platforms.
