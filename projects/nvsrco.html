NETWORK DEFENSE

Making network defense accessible

An open-source vulnerability-scanning platform that discovers hosts, scans ports with Nmap, runs OpenVAS assessments, and publishes results to an interactive dashboard.

NVSRCO · UPR Río Piedras · 2022–2025

MY ROLE

WHEN

View on GitHub ↗

The challenge

Organizations with limited security budgets need visibility into the devices on their networks, the services they expose, and the vulnerabilities that deserve attention. NVSRCO brings those tasks together using open-source tools.

What I built

I developed a scanning pipeline that discovers hosts, enumerates ports with Nmap, creates and runs OpenVAS assessment tasks, and exports findings as CSV reports. A Dash dashboard makes those results easier to explore.

Host discovery→Nmap→OpenVAS→CSV reports + Dash

Connecting remote networks

The published repository includes WireGuard scripts for connecting remote client networks, Docker configurations for the scanner and dashboard, and automation wrappers for the assessment stages. The assessment chain waits for each scan to finish before collecting its report.

Research output

The project grew out of my graduate research at UPR Río Piedras. The repository preserves the May 2025 defense version alongside the scanner, and the technical report is archived in the University of Puerto Rico institutional repository.
